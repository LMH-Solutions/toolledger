Sonix vs Rev (2026): pricing, AI vs human transcription, and who should use which

Research-based comparison. Prices and plan details read from sonix.ai/pricing, rev.com/pricing and rev.com/services/human-transcription on November 20, 2026 (US Eastern). Not hands-on tested. We have not measured either tool's accuracy. Prices are in USD and change; confirm on each vendor's page before you buy.

Sonix and Rev both turn recorded audio and video into text, and both show up when you search for a transcription service. The big difference is who does the transcribing and how you pay for it:

  • Sonix is an automated (AI) transcription platform for files you upload, with an in-browser editor, translation, subtitles and AI analysis. You pay per hour of audio: $10 an hour pay-as-you-go, or a monthly or yearly bundle of hours. Its pricing page doesn't list a human transcription service.
  • Rev sells both. AI transcription is $0.25 a minute pay-as-you-go or included in per-seat subscriptions, and human transcription starts at $1.99 a minute with a 99%+ accuracy guarantee and delivery in 12 hours or less (Rev's own claims). Rev's pricing page is now headed "Pricing Plans For Legal And Investigative Work", and its subscriptions are built around that audience.

Short version: if AI transcripts are good enough for you and you want translation or subtitles in many languages, Sonix is the simpler per-hour option. If you need a person to check the transcript (legal, research, hard audio), Rev is the one of the two that sells it. The prices and the math are below.

The key difference: AI only vs AI plus humans

Sonix's pricing page sells automated transcription only. You upload a file, get an AI transcript, and fix it yourself in the editor. Its plans are bundles of hours of audio.

Rev sells AI transcripts too, but its headline service is human transcription: a person transcribes the file, and Rev guarantees 99%+ accuracy. That costs far more per minute. Rev's AI subscriptions, on the other hand, include a lot of minutes per seat (5,000 a month on Essentials, about 83 hours).

So it comes down to two questions: do you need a human-checked transcript, and how many hours of audio do you have each month?

Which one should you pick?

  • Podcasters, video producers and creators who edit their own transcripts: Sonix. Per-hour pricing, 54+ languages, translation and subtitle exports on the same hours.
  • You need transcripts or subtitles in many languages: Sonix, unless you need human translators, which Rev sells as global subtitles.
  • Just a few files a year: Sonix Pay As You Go ($10/hr) is cheaper than Rev's pay-per-minute AI ($0.25/min = $15/hr). Rev's free plan covers 45 AI minutes a month if that's enough.
  • Lots of English or Spanish audio and AI is good enough: compare Rev Essentials ($25.49 to $29.99 per seat, 5,000 minutes) with Sonix Advanced or Pro; on list prices Rev Essentials costs less per hour at high volume.
  • You need a human-checked transcript (legal, research interviews, hard audio): Rev. Sonix doesn't list a human service.
  • Law firms and investigators: Rev's subscriptions are built for that work (page:line citations, legal-formatted transcripts, CJIS). Sonix also has a separate legal page, which we haven't covered here.

FAQ

Is Sonix cheaper than Rev?

For AI transcription paid per use, yes: Sonix is $10 per hour of audio and Rev is $0.25 a minute ($15 an hour). For heavy monthly AI use in English or Spanish, Rev's Essentials seat ($29.99 monthly, 5,000 minutes) costs less than Sonix's plans on list prices. Human transcription at Rev ($1.99/min and up) costs much more than either AI option.

Does Sonix offer human transcription?

Not on its pricing page as of November 20, 2026. Its plans cover automated transcription, translation, subtitles and AI analysis.

How fast is Rev?

Rev says AI transcripts arrive in five minutes or less and human transcripts in 12 hours or less, with rush delivery available for an extra fee (the rush price isn't shown on the pricing page).

Do Sonix's included hours roll over or scale with seats?

Sonix says included hours apply to the whole workspace, and extra seats don't add hours. Rev pools minutes across seats at the account level.

Is there a free option?

Sonix offers a 30-minute free trial. Rev has a Free plan with 45 AI transcription and caption minutes a month for one user, English only.
