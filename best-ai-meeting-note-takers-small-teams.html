Best AI meeting note-takers for small teams (2026): Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv and Fathom compared

Research-based comparison. All prices and features come from the official pricing pages: tl;dv and Fathom re-checked November 27, 2026; Fireflies.ai re-read November 26, 2026; Otter.ai checked November 19, 2026. Not hands-on tested. Vendors run promotions and change prices, so confirm at checkout.

If your team of 2 to 20 people wants an AI tool that joins calls, writes transcripts and sends summaries, four names come up most: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, tl;dv and Fathom. All four have a free plan and per-seat paid plans. They differ in what they limit: minutes, meeting length, number of users, or which features sit on the team tier.

We built this comparison only from what each company publishes on its pricing page. We haven't tested transcription accuracy, so we make no claims about which one is more accurate, and we don't give star ratings.

Otter.ai

Free (Basic): Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet support, live transcription, speaker identification, AI chat across meetings, iOS and Android apps, 300 monthly transcription minutes, a 30-minute cap per conversation and 3 lifetime audio/video file imports. Basic and Pro workspaces allow up to 5 members. The comparison table lists a cap of 20 AI Chat queries per user per month on the free plan.

Pro ($16.99 monthly, $8.33 annual): 1,200 in-app recording minutes, meetings up to 90 minutes, 10 file imports a month, team vocabulary, advanced search and export, and Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier integrations.

Business ($30 monthly, or $24 for the first 3 months for first-time subscribers; $19.99 annual): up to 25 workspace members, unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, meetings up to 4 hours, unlimited file imports, custom AI workflows, activity logs and usage analytics.

Watch for: video replay for Zoom and Google Meet is listed under Enterprise. SSO and SCIM need a minimum 100-user license. Otter lists transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese.

Fireflies.ai

Free: described as "free forever," with 400 minutes of storage per team, 20 AI credits and a 2-hour recording limit per meeting. Per Fireflies' Knowledge Base (read November 26, 2026), the free plan includes 3 meeting transcriptions by default; accounts created on or after September 1, 2026 get unlimited meeting transcriptions if Auto-join is turned on. Details: Fireflies free plan explained. Fireflies says paid tiers add more transcription credits, advanced exporting, and app and CRM integrations.

Pro ($18 monthly, $10 annual): Fireflies' FAQ says Pro and Business have "no rate limits for using the meeting bot or chrome extension on a monthly basis." Rate limits apply to the API, dialers and bulk uploads. Pro lists 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, 20 AI credits, a 2-hour recording limit and 720p video recording.

Business ($29 monthly, $19 annual): unlimited storage, 30 AI credits, a 3-hour recording limit, Multi-language Mode, conversation intelligence, team analytics and private channels. Enterprise is listed at $39 per seat, billed annually.

Watch for: every teammate added to the workspace counts as a seat (sharing a recap link with outsiders is free). Sign-in is through Google or Outlook only (SSO for enterprise). English is the main language, with Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian and "100+ other languages in beta." Volume discounts apply above 50 members.

tl;dv

tl;dv's pricing page (re-checked November 27, 2026) lists four tiers. With the Monthly toggle, Pro is $29 and Business $39 per seat per month; with the Annually toggle ("up to 40% off"), Pro is $18 and Business $29 per seat per month, billed annually. Enterprise is "Custom" (contact sales).

Free ($0, "No credit card required"): unlimited video recordings, unlimited transcription in 100+ languages, email and calendar integrations, and the iOS/Android app. AI use is capped: the plan card says 10 meetings with AI notes and "10x Ask AI queries" (the comparison table splits this into up to 5 single-meeting and up to 5 multi-meeting queries). The table also lists a data retention period of "up to 3 months*", up to 5 uploads, a 3-hour recording limit and a weekly web recording capacity of 40.

Pro ($29 monthly / $18 annual per seat): unlimited meetings with AI notes, premium transcription quality, multi-language support, custom AI notes templates, action item assignment, unlimited single-meeting Ask AI, email follow-up drafting, 5,000+ integrations, global transcript search, custom bot appearance, unlimited uploads and storage, and MCP server, API and webhooks. Weekly web recording capacity is listed as 80.

Business ($39 monthly / $29 annual per seat): adds advanced transcription, custom vocabulary, unlimited multi-meeting AI insights, scheduled AI reports, playbook monitoring, objection handling, and team-wide integrations and automations. Weekly web recording capacity is listed as 120.

Watch for: on November 27, 2026 the page showed a Black Friday banner: "500 coupons only: get an extra 45% off the Business annual plan" with the code BLACKFRIDAY45. We didn't apply it, so we can't confirm the final checkout price. SCIM, custom SSO and privately hosted AI are listed under Enterprise.

Fathom

Fathom's pricing page (re-checked November 27, 2026) has Individuals and Teams views and a Monthly / Annually toggle ("save 25%+").

Free (individuals): "free forever," with unlimited recordings and transcriptions, a choice of bot-free (beta, Mac) or bot capture, instant AI call summaries, and clips, playlists and search across calls. Some AI features, such as Ask Fathom and customizable summary templates, are marked "Limited use" on the free column of the comparison table.

Premium (individuals, $20 monthly / $16 annual): adds advanced call summaries, AI-generated action items, a conversational meeting assistant and MCP integrations with your LLM of choice.

Team ($19 monthly / $15 annual per user, 2-user minimum): unlimited meetings and AI summaries, a shared workspace (folders, comments, mentions, playlists), AI action items, trackers, search and Ask Fathom across team calls, team security controls, HIPAA compliance with a standard BAA, and "Unlimited MCP connected users."

Business ($34 monthly / $25 annual per user, 2-user minimum): adds unlimited CRM connected users, CRM field sync, Deal View, coaching metrics and AI scorecards, and custom summaries for your organization.

Enterprise ($35 per user per month, annual only): adds "Launch Assist" implementation, SSO and SCIM, custom data retention, custom BAA eligibility and a dedicated success manager.

Watch for: every plan card shows a "90 day guarantee." In the comparison table, the CRM syncs row shows "Max 3 users / domain" on some plans, and the MCP connections row says "Only for work domains; Max 5 users / domain." Check which limit applies to your plan before you rely on the free plan for several people.

Where each one is the better fit

  • Otter.ai fits teams that want a clearly stated free minute budget (300 minutes) and long meetings on the team tier (up to 4 hours on Business). Its listed transcription languages include Japanese and Chinese. It fits less well if you need video replay below Enterprise, or more than 5 people on Pro (Business allows up to 25).
  • Fireflies.ai fits small teams that want video recording and analytics on the team tier at $19/seat annual (just under Otter Business at $19.99; Fathom Team is lower at $15), and individuals who want no monthly meeting-bot limit on Pro. It fits less well if your team doesn't use Google or Outlook sign-in. See our Fireflies pricing guide for every plan's limits.
  • tl;dv fits people who want unlimited free recordings and transcription in 100+ languages and can live with AI notes on only 10 meetings and up to 3 months of data retention on the free plan. Its Business tier is the priciest team tier here on monthly billing ($39).
  • Fathom fits individuals who want unlimited free recordings and transcriptions, and small teams on a tight budget: Team is the lowest published team-tier price in this comparison ($15/user annual, 2-user minimum). Sales teams may look at Business for CRM field sync and coaching metrics.
