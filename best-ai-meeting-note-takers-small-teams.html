Best AI meeting note-takers for small teams (2026): Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv and Fathom compared

Research-based comparison. All prices and features come from the official pricing pages, checked November 3, 2026. Not hands-on tested. Vendors run promotions and change prices, so confirm at checkout.

If your team of 2 to 20 people wants an AI tool that joins calls, writes transcripts and sends summaries, four names come up most: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, tl;dv and Fathom. All four have a free plan and per-seat paid plans. They differ in what they limit: minutes, meeting length, number of users, or which features sit on the team tier.

We built this comparison only from what each company publishes on its pricing page. We haven't tested transcription accuracy, so we make no claims about which one is more accurate, and we don't give star ratings.

Otter.ai

Free (Basic): Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet support, live transcription, speaker identification, AI chat across meetings, iOS and Android apps, 300 monthly transcription minutes and 3 lifetime audio/video file imports. The comparison table lists a cap of 20 AI Chat queries per user per month on the free plan.

Pro ($16.99 monthly, $8.33 annual): 1,200 in-app recording minutes, meetings up to 90 minutes, 10 file imports a month, team vocabulary, advanced search and export, and Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier integrations.

Business ($30 monthly, $24 annual): unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, meetings up to 4 hours, unlimited file imports, custom AI workflows, activity logs and usage analytics.

Watch for: video replay for Zoom and Google Meet is listed under Enterprise. SSO and SCIM need a minimum 100-user license. Otter lists transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese.

Fireflies.ai

Free: described as "free forever." Fireflies says paid tiers add more transcription credits, advanced exporting, and app and CRM integrations.

Pro ($18 monthly, $10 annual): Fireflies' FAQ says Pro and Business have "no rate limits for using the meeting bot or chrome extension on a monthly basis." Rate limits apply to the API, dialers and bulk uploads. Pro can send audio for transcription through Zapier.

Business ($29 monthly, $19 annual): higher team-pooled API limits, analytics, private channels and video recording. Enterprise is listed at $39 per seat, billed annually.

Watch for: every teammate added to the workspace counts as a seat (sharing a recap link with outsiders is free). Sign-in is through Google or Outlook only (SSO for enterprise). English is the main language, with Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian and "100+ other languages in beta." Volume discounts apply above 50 members.

tl;dv

tl;dv's pricing page publishes three self-serve tiers in euros: Free (€0), Pro at €29 per seat monthly or €18 per seat per month billed annually (€216 a year), and Business at €39 monthly or €29 per seat per month billed annually (€348 a year). The page advertises "40% off on every annual plan."

The feature table on tl;dv's page loads through scripts that we couldn't read reliably today, so we don't list per-plan feature limits here. Check them on tldv.io/pricing before you buy.

Fathom

Free (individuals): "free forever," with unlimited recordings and transcriptions, a choice of bot-free (beta, Mac) or bot capture, instant AI call summaries, and clips, playlists and search across calls.

Premium (individuals, $20 monthly / $16 annual): adds advanced call summaries, AI action items, a conversational meeting assistant and a custom meeting bot.

Team ($19 monthly / $15 annual per user, 2-user minimum): adds global search across calls, team highlight playlists, comments, folders and keyword alerts.

Business ($34 monthly / $25 annual per user, 2-user minimum): adds CRM field sync, Deal View, coaching metrics, AI scorecards and custom summaries. SSO, SCIM and HIPAA BAA are on Enterprise (talk to sales).

Watch for: the pricing page lists a "90 day guarantee" on plans and a "Max 3 users/domain" note in the comparison table. Check how that applies to your team before you rely on the free plan for several people.

Where each one is the better fit

  • Otter.ai fits teams that want a clearly stated free minute budget (300 minutes) and long meetings on the team tier (up to 4 hours on Business). Its listed transcription languages include Japanese and Chinese. It fits less well if you need video replay below Enterprise.
  • Fireflies.ai fits small teams that want video recording and analytics at the lowest published annual team-tier price among the US-dollar tools here ($19/seat), and individuals who want no monthly meeting-bot limit on Pro. It fits less well if your team doesn't use Google or Outlook sign-in.
  • tl;dv fits teams already budgeting in euros. Verify features on its pricing page first, since we couldn't confirm them today.
  • Fathom fits individuals who want unlimited free recordings and transcriptions, and small teams on a tight budget: Team is the lowest published team-tier price in this comparison ($15/user annual, 2-user minimum). Sales teams may look at Business for CRM field sync and coaching metrics.
