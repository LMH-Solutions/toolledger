Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai pricing breakdown (2026): what each plan really costs

Research-based pricing analysis. Sources: otter.ai/pricing and fireflies.ai/pricing, both re-read on November 19, 2026; Fireflies free-plan details from its Knowledge Base, read November 26, 2026. Not hands-on tested. Both vendors run promotions, so confirm prices at checkout.

Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are two of the best-known AI meeting note-takers. Both can join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, transcribe them and write summaries. Both charge per seat, have free plans, and give a large discount for annual billing. But the plans limit different things, so the "cheapest" option depends on how long your meetings run, how many people need seats and whether you need video or analytics.

This breakdown covers only what each company publishes on its pricing page. We haven't compared transcription accuracy hands-on, so we make no claims about which one is more accurate.

Free plans compared

Otter Basic (free)

Otter's free Basic plan includes the Zoom, Teams and Google Meet integrations, live transcription, speaker identification, audio playback, the iOS and Android apps, AI chat within and across meetings, AI meeting workflows, and the Otter MCP server. The limits Otter publishes are 300 monthly transcription minutes (a shared pool for meetings and imports, no rollover), 30 minutes per conversation, 3 lifetime audio/video file imports and the 25 most recent conversations in your history. The comparison table also lists a monthly cap on AI Chat queries ("20 per user, 3 per conversation") in the free column.

Fireflies Free

Fireflies describes its Free plan as "free forever." The plan card lists unlimited transcription (with an asterisk), limited AI summaries, 400 minutes of storage per team and 20 AI credits, and the comparison table lists a 2-hour recording limit per meeting. Its pricing FAQ says Pro and Business plans come with more transcription credits and advanced exporting, and that app and CRM integrations are only on premium tiers. So Fireflies limits the free plan by storage and credits, not by a monthly minute count.

The "unlimited" asterisk has a condition. Per Fireflies' Knowledge Base (read November 26, 2026), Free includes 3 meeting transcriptions by default, and accounts created on or after September 1, 2026 get unlimited meeting transcriptions only with Auto-join turned on. See Fireflies free plan explained for the details.

Takeaway: Otter's free plan resets 300 minutes every month but stops each conversation at 30 minutes. Fireflies' free plan allows meetings up to 2 hours, but all saved meetings share 400 minutes of team storage, so you'll need to delete old ones to keep going, and unless Auto-join is on (new accounts from September 1, 2026) it transcribes only 3 meetings by default.

Entry paid plans: Otter Pro vs Fireflies Pro

Otter Pro costs $16.99 per user per month on monthly billing. With annual billing it's $8.33 per user per month, and Otter advertises that as a 51% saving. Pro includes 1,200 in-app recording minutes, meetings of up to 90 minutes, 10 audio/video file imports per month, advanced meeting templates, team vocabulary and taggable speakers, advanced search, export and playback, and Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier integrations. Export formats on Pro include mp3, txt, pdf, docx and srt.

Fireflies Pro costs $18 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $10 per seat per month billed annually. Fireflies' FAQ says Pro and Business users have "no rate limits for using the meeting bot or chrome extension on a monthly basis." Rate limits apply to some other routes, such as the API, dialers and bulk file uploads. The Pro plan card lists unlimited AI summaries, 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, 20 AI credits, video recording (720p in the comparison table) and downloads of transcripts, summaries and recordings.

What this means in practice:

  • On annual billing, Otter Pro ($8.33) costs less than Fireflies Pro ($10) per seat. On monthly billing, Otter Pro ($16.99) is a little cheaper than Fireflies Pro ($18).
  • Otter Pro has two hard limits that can matter: 1,200 minutes and 90 minutes per meeting. If you often run calls longer than 90 minutes, Otter Pro's published cap rules it out. Fireflies' FAQ describes no monthly rate limit for the meeting bot on Pro, though its plan table (checked November 19, 2026) lists a 2-hour recording limit per meeting on Pro, 3 hours on Business and 4 hours on Enterprise.

Team plans: Otter Business vs Fireflies Business

Otter Business is $30 per user per month on monthly billing ($24 for the first 3 months for first-time subscribers) or $19.99 per user per month billed annually. Workspaces can have up to 25 members (Basic and Pro allow up to 5). It includes unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, meetings of up to 4 hours, unlimited file imports (the table lists 6,000 imported-file minutes per user per month), custom AI workflows, 3 concurrent meetings, and admin features such as activity logs and usage analytics.

Fireflies Business is $29 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $19 billed annually. The plan card lists unlimited storage, 30 AI credits, Multi-language Mode, conversation intelligence, team analytics for admins, public meeting access and user groups, with a 3-hour recording limit per meeting.

Video note: As of November 19, 2026, Fireflies lists video recording starting on Pro (720p in its comparison table; 1080p on Enterprise). Otter lists "Video replay for Zoom and Google Meet" under Enterprise. If your team needs to watch meeting video and not just read the transcript, that difference matters.

Hidden costs and limits to check

  • Workspace size: Otter lists a maximum of 5 workspace members on Basic and Pro, 25 on Business and unlimited on Enterprise. A team of 6 or more needs Business.
  • Seats: Fireflies counts every teammate added to the workspace as a seat. You can share individual meeting recap links with people outside your team for free.
  • Sign-in: Fireflies says sign-up is only through Google or Outlook authentication (or SSO for enterprise). If your team uses another email provider, check this before you buy.
  • Add-ons: Fireflies sells a custom notetaker bot name as a paid add-on on Pro and Business. Otter lists HIPAA compliance as an Enterprise add-on, and live transcription via RTMP as having additional fees.
  • Languages: Otter lists AI transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese. Fireflies says English is its primary language, with Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian and "100+ other languages in beta."
  • SSO: Otter lists SSO and SCIM under Enterprise with a minimum 100-user license. Fireflies lists SSO and SCIM in its higher tiers.
  • Discounts: Otter offers 20% off Pro for students and teachers with a .edu email ($79.99/year or $13.59/month as published). Fireflies says volume discounts are available for more than 50 members.

Security and compliance (as published)

Fireflies says it is SOC 2 Type II compliant, GDPR compliant and ISO 42001 certified. It also says HIPAA BAAs are available on Enterprise with Private Storage, it encrypts data with AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit, and customer data isn't used for AI training. Otter lists TLS encryption, AES-256 for real-time content, two-factor authentication on every plan and HIPAA as an Enterprise add-on. Regulated organizations should ask both vendors for the actual compliance documents rather than relying on marketing pages.

Which one costs less for you?

  • A solo user with short meetings who pays yearly: Otter Pro has the lowest published annual price ($8.33/month), as long as 1,200 minutes and 90-minute meetings are enough.
  • A solo user with long or frequent meetings: Fireflies Pro ($10/month annual) has no monthly limit on bot recordings according to its FAQ.
  • A small team that wants video recordings: Fireflies Pro ($10/seat annual) and Business ($19/seat annual) list video recording. Otter puts video replay on Enterprise.
  • A team with long meetings that needs admin analytics: Otter Business ($19.99/seat annual, 4-hour meetings, unlimited recordings) and Fireflies Business ($19/seat annual, 3-hour recording limit, unlimited storage) cost almost the same, so let meeting length and video decide.
