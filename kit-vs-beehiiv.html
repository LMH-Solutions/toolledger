Kit vs beehiiv (2026): pricing, free plans and paid-newsletter fees compared

Research-based comparison. Prices and plan details read from Kit's and beehiiv's own pricing and help pages on November 17, 2026. Not hands-on tested. Prices change; confirm on kit.com/pricing and beehiiv.com/pricing before you decide.

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) and beehiiv are two of the most popular platforms for creators who want to grow a newsletter and, often, charge for it. Both have a free plan. Both sell paid plans priced by list size. They differ in where the free plan stops, what it costs to turn on paid subscriptions, and which extras come bundled. This guide puts the published numbers side by side.

Everything here comes from the two companies' published pages. We haven't run a publication on either platform for this article, so it compares what they publish, not how they perform.

The short answer

  • Kit Free is $0 up to 10,000 subscribers, and Kit says paid newsletters and digital products work on every plan, including Free, at 3.5% + $0.30 per USD transaction. Email sequences and visual automations need a paid plan (Creator starts at $39 a month at 1,000 subscribers).
  • beehiiv Free is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends, but its plan table shows paid subscriptions, automations and the Ad Network starting on Lite, which is $59 a month (or $49 a month billed annually) at up to 1,000 subscribers. beehiiv says it takes 0% of paid subscription revenue; Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 still applies.
  • On published monthly prices, Kit is the same price or cheaper than beehiiv at every list size we compared below, and much cheaper for a small paid newsletter, because you can charge on Kit Free. beehiiv bundles more into its paid plans: a website builder, podcast hosting, an ad network and community features.

Per payment, beehiiv's paid plans keep slightly more because beehiiv adds no fee of its own. The bigger difference is the plan you need to charge at all. Take 1,000 subscribers with 50 paying $5 a month ($250 gross):

  • Kit Free: about $23.75 in fees, so you keep about $226 a month. If you also want automated sequences, Kit Creator at $39 brings that to about $187.
  • beehiiv Lite (monthly): $59 plan plus about $22.25 in Stripe fees, so you keep about $169 a month (automations included). Annual Lite at $49 a month brings that to about $179.

The gap narrows as your list and paid revenue grow, and the two are priced the same at 10,000 and 25,000 subscribers on the entry plans. Run the numbers with your own price, list size and features.

What each one bundles

Kit

Kit's help center lists on Free: up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited forms and landing pages, unlimited broadcasts, tagging and segmentation, digital products and subscriptions, A/B testing on broadcasts, free and Smart Recommendations through the Creator Network, and API access. Paid plans add visual automations and the automation template library, email sequences, rules and RSS campaigns, AI Connectors, paid apps in the Kit App Store, and earning from Paid Recommendations.

beehiiv

beehiiv's pricing page lists on Free: newsletters, a website and a podcast, campaign analytics, unlimited sends, its Recommendation Network, link-in-bio, an AI website builder and read-only API and MCP access. Lite adds the Ad Network, paid recommendations, 0% take rate on paid subscriptions, digital products, community, email automations (up to 10 live), surveys and polls, webhooks and 2 team seats. Pro adds removing beehiiv branding, a sponsorship storefront, audio newsletters, RSS to send, up to 10 publications, 10 seats, dynamic content, the Send API and click-triggered automations. beehiiv notes some Lite and Pro features, including the Ad Network and the 0% take rate, aren't available during its trial, and some aren't available in all regions.

Who should pick which

  • Pick Kit if you want to start charging (or selling digital products) without a monthly bill, your list may pass 2,500 subscribers before you're ready to pay for software, or you want automated sequences at the lowest entry price ($39 vs $59 at 1,000 subscribers).
  • Pick beehiiv if you want a newsletter website, podcast hosting, community and an ad network in one place, and you're fine paying for Lite or Pro to unlock paid subscriptions and automations.
  • If you're unsure, price both at the list size and paid revenue you realistically expect a year from now, using the tables above.

FAQ

Can I run a paid newsletter on beehiiv's free plan? Not according to beehiiv's plan comparison table, which shows paid subscriptions starting on Lite.

Does beehiiv take a cut of paid subscriptions? beehiiv's FAQ says it takes 0% of paid subscription revenue, and you pay Stripe's standard processing fee of 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.

Can I run a paid newsletter on Kit's free plan? Yes. Kit's help center says selling in USD costs 3.5% + $0.30 per transaction with no monthly fee for Commerce, and that this is the only cost of selling through Kit.
