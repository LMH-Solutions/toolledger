Fireflies.ai pricing explained (2026): Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise per seat

Research-based guide. Prices and plan details read from Fireflies' live pricing page on November 11, 2026 (US Eastern). Not hands-on tested. Prices are in USD and change; confirm on fireflies.ai/pricing before you buy.

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting note-taker: a bot (or a Chrome extension, desktop app or mobile app) records your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls, transcribes them and writes summaries. It has four plans: Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise. Paid plans are priced per seat, and the annual price is much lower than the monthly one, so the real answer to "how much does Fireflies cost?" depends on how many people need a seat and how you pay.

Everything here comes from Fireflies' own pricing page and FAQ. We haven't run a paid Fireflies workspace for this article, so it covers what Fireflies publishes, not how well the transcripts or summaries perform.

What the free plan gives you

Fireflies calls Free "free forever." The plan card lists unlimited transcription (with an asterisk), limited AI summaries, 400 minutes of storage for the whole team and 20 AI credits. It includes the meeting bot for Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and more, transcription in 100+ languages, real-time notes, meeting search, the AskFred AI assistant (limited), file uploads, the desktop, mobile and Chrome extension apps, and API access at 30 requests a minute and 500 a day.

The catch is storage. 400 minutes per team is roughly seven hour-long meetings kept at once (our arithmetic). Free also doesn't include video recording or downloads of transcripts, summaries and recordings, which start on Pro.

Pro: the plan most individuals need

Pro is $10 per seat per month billed annually ($120 a year for one person) or $18 billed monthly. The plan card adds unlimited AI summaries, 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, video recording (the comparison table lists 720p), downloads, the Personal Assistant and Email Assistant, AI Skills, Voice Agents, an action-item and task manager, and unlimited integrations. API limits rise to 60 a minute and 5,000 a day.

Two limits to know: the recording limit is listed as 2 hours per meeting, the same as Free, and AI credits stay at 20. If your calls regularly run past two hours, look at Business.

Business: for teams

Business is $19 per seat per month billed annually or $29 billed monthly, and Fireflies marks it "most popular." It adds unlimited storage, a 3-hour recording limit, 30 AI credits, Multi-language Mode, conversation intelligence (topic trackers, sentiment, speaker talk-time and similar analytics), team analytics for admins, public meeting access, user groups, and unlimited private as well as public channels. API limits become 60 a minute per paid seat, pooled, up to 1,200 a minute.

Enterprise: compliance and admin controls

Enterprise is listed at $39 per seat per month, annual only. It adds a rules engine, SSO and SCIM, audit logs via the API, private storage, custom data retention, a transcript-and-summary-only mode, a super admin role, a dedicated account manager (with an asterisk on the page), 1080p video, a 4-hour recording limit and 50 AI credits. Fireflies says it is HIPAA-ready on Enterprise with a BAA.

Things to check before you buy

  • Sign-in: Fireflies' FAQ says sign-up is only through Google or Outlook authentication (SSO on request). Check this if your team uses another email provider.
  • Rate limits: the FAQ says there are no monthly rate limits on the meeting bot or Chrome extension for Pro and Business, but the API, dialers and bulk file uploads do have limits.
  • Languages: English is the primary language. Fireflies lists Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian and 100+ other languages as being in beta.
  • Bot name: customizing the notetaker bot's name is a paid add-on on Pro and Business.
  • Security: Fireflies says it is SOC 2 Type II compliant, GDPR compliant and ISO 42001 certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and doesn't train AI models on your meeting data. Ask for the actual documents if you're in a regulated field.

Which Fireflies plan fits

  • Trying it out, or a few meetings a month: Free, as long as you can live within 400 minutes of storage and no video.
  • A solo professional or a small team that wants downloads and video: Pro on annual billing ($10 per seat per month).
  • A team with calls over two hours, or managers who want analytics: Business ($19 per seat per month annually).
  • Organizations that need SSO, HIPAA or retention controls: Enterprise.
