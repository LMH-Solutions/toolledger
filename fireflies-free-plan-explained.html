Fireflies free plan explained (2026): the 3-meeting limit, Auto-join "unlimited" transcripts, storage and AI credits

Research-based guide. Everything here was read from Fireflies' live pricing page (plan cards, comparison table and FAQ) and Fireflies Knowledge Base articles (US English, USD) on November 26, 2026 (US Eastern). Not hands-on tested: we haven't used a Fireflies free account for this article, and the limits below are Fireflies' own descriptions. Plans change, so confirm on fireflies.ai/pricing before you rely on them.

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting note-taker. A bot, Chrome extension, desktop app or mobile app records your calls, transcribes them and writes summaries. Its Free plan is labelled "free forever," and the pricing card says "Unlimited transcription*." The asterisk matters. Fireflies' help articles say new free accounts get 3 meeting transcriptions by default, and that "unlimited" applies only when you turn on Auto-join. This guide explains how that works, what else Free limits, where Fireflies' own pages disagree, and when upgrading makes sense. How "unlimited" transcription actually works on Free

Fireflies' Knowledge Base article "Unlock Unlimited Transcriptions on the Fireflies Free Plan" (marked last updated about two months ago) sets the rules:

  • Auto-join ON: Fireflies joins and transcribes your eligible meetings "with no cap on the number of transcriptions."
  • Auto-join OFF: you're limited to "the Free plan's default of 3 meeting transcriptions."
  • Which meetings count: a dropdown filters what Auto-join picks up: all meetings with a web-conference link, only meetings you own, or only meetings with teammates. The option "Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai" does not support unlimited transcriptions. Inviting the bot by hand still transcribes that one meeting, but it doesn't unlock unlimited transcription.
  • Where it works: the article says unlimited transcripts cover Google Meet through the Chrome extension, in-person recordings in the mobile app, and shared-bot meetings. The pricing page's tooltip for the asterisk says the same: "For shared bot meetings, chrome extension, and mobile app."
  • Transcripts, not AI features: Fireflies is explicit that unlimited transcripts give you the raw, searchable text. Its help article says AI features such as summaries, meeting recaps, action items and analytics "still depend on your plan's AI credit balance."

In practice, Free is generous only if you're comfortable letting the bot auto-join your calendar meetings. If you'd rather invite it to specific calls, plan around 3 transcriptions.

Storage: the limit you'll hit first

Free includes 400 minutes of stored meetings. Fireflies' storage article says storage is "the total minutes of meetings you can store—not per month." By our arithmetic, that's just under seven hour-long meetings kept at once. When you reach the limit, Fireflies says you need to delete older files to store new ones. Pro raises storage to 8,000 minutes per seat, and Business and Enterprise are unlimited.

Unlimited transcription with a 400-minute storage cap means you'll be deleting old meetings regularly if you record a lot. That matters more because Free can't download transcripts. You can't simply export old meetings before deleting them.

AI credits and summaries

  • 20 AI credits. Fireflies' "AI Credits Pricing and Overview" guide says every account gets one-time credits by plan: Free 20 on sign-up, Pro 20, Business 30, Enterprise 50. It says plan credits don't expire, are shared across the team, and don't increase with added seats.
  • What uses credits: AI Skills (custom notes from templates), AI soundbites, custom summary sections, Voice Agents, Sales Assist, the Slack Assistant, CRM autofill, AskFred during live meetings, and the Personal Assistant's Daily Digest and Meeting Prep. Fireflies says charges are per query and vary with complexity, so it doesn't publish a fixed number of uses per credit.
  • Watch the Personal Assistant. The guide says Daily Digest and Meeting Prep are on by default and consume AI credits. If you want your 20 credits for something else, turn them off in AI Skills settings.
  • Running out on Free: Fireflies says free users see a paywall and an upgrade prompt, with "no auto-charge." Buying extra credits is a paid-plan add-on (from $5 a month for 50 credits).
  • Summaries: the pricing page lists "Limited AI summaries" for Free. Its help pages describe summaries as using credits, but they disagree about which kind (see the next section).

Where Fireflies' own pages disagree

We found several conflicts between Fireflies' pricing page and its Knowledge Base on November 26, 2026. We're reporting them as published, not guessing which is right:

  • Transcription: the Free plan card says "Unlimited transcription*," while the comparison table on the same page lists Free's transcript allowance as "Limited." The help articles explain the gap: 3 meetings by default, unlimited with Auto-join.
  • How many free transcriptions: "What's included in the Fireflies Free plan" (updated about a week ago) lists sign-up transcription credits by source: 3 from the website, 5 from the Chrome extension, 10 for a new mobile-app user, 5 for an existing mobile-app user. Other articles simply say 3 meetings.
  • AI credits, monthly or one-time: the same Free plan article says "20 AI credits per month." The "Free Plan Features" article and the AI-credit guide say the 20 credits are granted once. The comparison table leaves Free's AI-credit cell blank, while the plan card lists 20.
  • AI summaries: the pricing page says "Limited AI summaries." The "Free Plan Features" article opens with "unlimited transcription and unlimited AI summaries on the Free plan," then says summaries are "generated using your available AI credits." The Free plan article says summarizing a meeting uses a transcription credit, and the AI-credit guide says recap emails are covered by your plan and not charged in AI credits.
  • Storage per team or per seat: the pricing page says "400 mins of storage/team." The Free plan articles say "400 minutes of storage per seat," and the storage article's table heads the column "Storage per user."
  • Integrations: the Free plan article says "All integrations are available on the Free plan" (50+ apps). The pricing comparison table leaves Free's integrations cell blank and shows "All" from Pro up. The plan cards list "Unlimited integrations" as a Pro feature, and the pricing FAQ says app and CRM integrations come "on the premium tiers."
  • Video recording: the comparison table lists 720p video from Pro, but the pricing FAQ says it's the Business tier that "gets ... video recording."

If one of these limits decides whether Free works for you, check it in the app or ask Fireflies support (support@fireflies.ai, listed on the pricing page) before you build a workflow around it.

Free plan vs the 7-day free trial

Fireflies also offers a separate trial, and it works very differently from Free:

  • 7 days of Business features. The trial article (marked last updated about seven months ago) says the trial starts on the Business plan by default.
  • A card is required. "A valid credit/debit card is required to activate it." Today's charge shows $0.00.
  • It converts automatically. If you don't cancel before day 7, your card is charged for the plan you selected (monthly is the default billing option). You can downgrade the trial to Pro first.
  • Cancelling returns you to Free immediately, and Fireflies says your meetings, transcripts and summaries stay accessible. Each account gets the trial only once.
  • The AI-credit trial is a separate add-on. If you also start the optional AI credits (AskFred) trial, Fireflies says you're auto-subscribed to the credit plan you picked unless you cancel it separately.

Our read: if you just want to see whether Fireflies' transcripts are useful, Free with Auto-join doesn't require card details, according to Fireflies' help pages. Start the trial only when you're ready to judge the Business features, and set a reminder for day 6.

When to upgrade (and to what)

  • You keep hitting 400 minutes, or need downloads or video: Pro, at $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 monthly. It adds 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, downloads, 720p video and unlimited AI summaries. Note that it has the same 2-hour recording cap and the same 20 AI credits as Free.
  • Your meetings run over 2 hours, or you need unlimited storage, analytics or private channels: Business, at $19 per seat per month billed annually or $29 monthly (3-hour cap, 30 AI credits).
  • You need SSO, HIPAA (with a BAA) or retention controls: Enterprise, $39 per seat per month, annual only.

Fireflies counts every teammate added to your workspace as a seat. Its FAQ says you can share individual meeting recap links with people outside your team for free. The full tier tables and team-cost math are in our Fireflies pricing guide.

Who the free plan suits

  • Good fit: individuals who want searchable transcripts of calendar meetings, don't mind auto-join, and can live with deleting old meetings and not exporting them.
  • Workable: people recording in-person conversations on the mobile app, or Google Meet users on the Chrome extension, both listed as covered by unlimited transcripts.
  • Poor fit: anyone who needs to download transcripts, record video, keep a long archive, or run regular meetings over 2 hours. Also anyone who only wants the bot in specific meetings, since manual invites don't unlock unlimited transcription.
