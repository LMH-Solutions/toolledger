About ToolLedger

ToolLedger is a small comparison site about software for creators, solo businesses and small teams. Right now we cover email marketing platforms and AI meeting note-takers.

How we research

  • We research. We have not tested these tools ourselves. Our current articles come from the vendors' official pricing pages, help docs, terms and public announcements. We link every source. We do not claim to have tested a product unless we actually did, and if we do test one, the article will say so and describe how we tested.
  • No fake ratings or testimonials. We don't make up star ratings, user quotes or statistics. If we quote a number, it comes from a source we link.
  • We date our facts. Prices change often. Each article says when we checked the sources. Please confirm the current price on the vendor's site before you buy.
  • Affiliate links. Some links may earn us a commission. See our affiliate disclosure. If one tool pays us more than another, we still recommend the one that fits your situation better.
