Sonix review (2026): pricing, features and who it's for

Research-based review. Prices and plan features copied from sonix.ai/pricing, checked November 9, 2026 (America/Denver). Not hands-on tested. We make no independent accuracy claims. Prices are in USD and can change; confirm on Sonix before you buy.

Sonix is an AI speech-to-text platform aimed at people who upload audio or video files and need a transcript, translation, subtitles or AI analysis afterward. That is a different job from meeting bots such as Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai, which join Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams live. If your main need is "I have a recording, get me a searchable transcript," Sonix is in that category. If your main need is "join my calls and write the notes," start with our Otter vs Fireflies pricing breakdown or the small-team meeting note-taker comparison.

This page only reports what Sonix publishes. We have not measured transcription quality ourselves, and we do not invent accuracy percentages, star ratings or testimonials.

How Sonix bills hours

Sonix's pricing FAQ says "per hour" means the length of the audio or video file you upload, not how long transcription takes and not how long you sit in the app. Charges are prorated to the nearest second. Translation, alignment and burned-in subtitles draw from the same transcription-hour pool on subscription plans (on Pay As You Go you buy more hours). AI analysis hours work the same way: they measure the length of recordings you run AI on.

Every new account includes 30 free minutes with no credit card required. Free minutes are applied to the first upload before purchased minutes.

What every plan includes (as published)

Sonix says all plans include a powerful in-browser editor, transcription and translation in 54+ languages, speaker labels and timestamps, automated subtitles and captions, a custom dictionary, visual transcripts, search across transcripts, speaker voiceprint, version history, a medical transcription model, an AI meeting recorder, and exports such as DOCX, PDF, TXT, SRT and VTT. The comparison table also lists shareable transcript links, an embeddable media player, burn-in subtitles, API access, Zapier and webhook events.

Team and admin features (multi-user access, permissions, shared folders, billing administrator, per-user quotas) sit on the subscription plans rather than Pay As You Go. Security items listed for the product include SSL and at-rest encryption, two-factor authentication and a SOC 2 Type 2 report. Enterprise adds SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs, retention controls, HIPAA with a BAA, GDPR/DPA, custom hours and volume pricing.

Accuracy: what Sonix claims vs what we verified

Sonix's marketing and pricing pages claim 99% transcription accuracy and "award-winning accuracy." That is the vendor's claim. We did not run our own accuracy tests, so we cannot confirm, dispute or refine that figure. Treat accuracy as something to check with your own sample files during the free 30-minute trial.

Who Sonix fits (and who should look elsewhere)

  • Occasional file uploads: Pay As You Go at $10/hr avoids a monthly subscription when you only transcribe now and then.
  • Steady solo or small-team volume: Core ($25/mo, 5 hrs) or Advanced ($50/mo, 20 hrs) packages hours plus AI workspace usage and more storage. Extra seats are +$25/mo each and, per Sonix, do not add more hours.
  • High volume or regulated work: Pro (40 hrs/mo) or Enterprise (custom; Sonix lists SOC 2, HIPAA BAA, GDPR, SSO). Sonix also markets legal and healthcare solutions on the same pricing page.
  • Live meeting notes as the main job: Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv or Fathom are purpose-built meeting bots. Sonix lists an "AI meeting recorder," but its published pricing is built around uploaded hours, not per-seat meeting minutes the way Otter and Fireflies are. Compare those tools first if calls are your workflow.
  • Subtitles and multilingual translation in one upload: Sonix emphasizes 54+ languages for both transcription and translation, plus SRT/VTT and burn-in. That is a stronger published fit for media/production workflows than a typical meeting note-taker.
