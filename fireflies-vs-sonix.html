Fireflies vs Sonix (2026): meeting note-taker or transcription platform? Pricing compared

Research-based comparison. Prices and plan details read from fireflies.ai/pricing and sonix.ai/pricing on November 13, 2026 (US Eastern). Not hands-on tested. We have not measured either tool's accuracy. Prices are in USD and change; confirm on each vendor's page before you buy.

Fireflies.ai and Sonix both turn speech into text, so they often show up in the same searches. They are built for different jobs, though, and they charge in completely different ways:

  • Fireflies.ai is a meeting note-taker. A bot (or the Chrome extension, desktop or mobile app) records your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls, then writes a transcript and AI summary. Paid plans are per seat, and transcription is listed as unlimited on every plan.
  • Sonix is a transcription platform for files you upload: interviews, podcasts, videos, recorded meetings. It adds an in-browser editor, translation, subtitles and AI analysis. Pricing is based on hours of audio: $10 an hour pay-as-you-go, or a monthly bundle of hours.

So the short answer: if your audio is mostly live meetings, Fireflies' per-seat model is usually far cheaper per hour. If you mostly transcribe recorded files and need translation, subtitles or careful editing, Sonix is in that category. The details and the math are below.

The key difference: per seat vs per hour

With Fireflies you pay for people. One Pro seat costs the same whether you record two meetings a month or forty; what limits you is storage (8,000 minutes per Pro seat, unlimited on Business), a 2-hour cap per recording on Pro, and AI credits.

With Sonix you pay for audio. Every hour you upload counts against your plan's hours, and anything past them is $10 an hour. Adding teammates costs extra but doesn't add hours.

That is why the right choice depends mostly on how many hours of audio you have and where it comes from.

Which one should you pick?

  • You want notes from your Zoom, Meet or Teams calls: Fireflies. Start on Free, move to Pro ($10 per seat annually) when you need video, downloads or more storage.
  • A team that meets all day: Fireflies Pro or Business. Per-seat pricing with unlimited transcription beats paying per hour.
  • You transcribe interviews, podcasts or videos you already recorded: Sonix, especially if you need subtitles, translation or a careful editing pass.
  • Only a few files a year: Sonix Pay As You Go ($10/hr, no subscription).
  • Both: some people use Fireflies for meetings and Sonix for content production. Check the free options first so you don't pay twice for the same job.
